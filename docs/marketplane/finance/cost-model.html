That single asymmetry decides the packaging, the pricing, the hiring plan and the shape of the P&L — and it is the reason the entire data plane can be given away without it being a loss leader.

01 — The finding

Give away the plumbing. Charge for the advice.

Every piece of infrastructure this product runs on is priced per request, per gigabyte or per million operations — and at SME volumes those units round to nothing. The language model does not.

A customer with four connectors, nightly restatement pulls, a year of history in Postgres and every raw payload archived in object storage costs about ฿1.65 a month to serve. The same customer asking fifteen questions of the advice surface costs ฿148.

90:1

The ratio between what a customer's judgement costs and what their data costs. It does not improve with scale, because neither side has a fixed component worth amortising — which means packaging, not engineering, is what decides the margin.

That has one clean consequence. The dashboard, the connectors, the after-fees number, the full history, the exports — all of it is nearly free to serve, so it can be genuinely free to use. What costs money is the model reading your numbers and telling you what to do about them. Meter that, and only that.

The generous tier is the honest tier

A free plan that hands over the entire data plane — every connector, unlimited history, the whole after-fees dashboard — costs ฿1.65 per user per month and never feels mean, because nothing is being withheld to manufacture pressure. The paid tiers start where the real cost starts: the weekly action sheet, the daily brief, and the questions.

A Starter customer who asks fifteen questions a month against a hundred-question allowance will never once see a limit. That is what generous means operationally: the cap exists to stop abuse, not to shape behaviour. It is also the distribution strategy — §07 shows the free base reaching 34,800 users against 1,995 paying ones, at a serving cost of ฿57,000 a month — less than one and a half customer-success salaries.

Enterprise is a band, not a price

Above Multi-unit the product stops having a price and starts having a contract, but it does not jump straight to a large one. The Revenue Department will pay 15–20% of your customer’s bill, if you are listed. A regulation effective 7 February 2026 gives Thai SMEs — paid-up capital under ฿5M and revenue under ฿30M, which is exactly this customer — a 200% corporate income tax deduction on software subscriptions and digital services, capped at ฿300,000 of additional exemption per accounting period and running to 31 December 2027 verified. A Starter customer deducts ฿38,160 against ฿19,080 paid; at the 15–20% SME rate that is ฿2,862–3,816 back, an effective 15–20% discount funded by the state. The condition is that the vendor is registered on DEPA’s Thailand Digital Catalog — purchases from unregistered vendors do not qualify at all. Registering is a form, it is the single cheapest act of pricing power available here, and the window closes at the end of 2027.

Blended ARPU is ฿3,356, and that is the number to argue about

At a 60 / 30 / 10 mix the blended price is ฿3,356 net per customer per month $102, so a thousand SME customers is ฿40M of ARR and not a baht more. The margin is excellent and the margin is not the constraint — ARPU is. Every meaningful increase in enterprise value comes from moving the mix up, not from shaving cost, and the ceiling is where that happens: by month 36 twenty-five enterprise logos are 1.2% of customers and 18% of revenue, while Multi-unit is 10% of customers and 32%. Together, an eighth of the logos produce half the revenue.

Cloudflare AI Gateway

Worth adding, and it is already on the stack. Core features — analytics, caching, rate limiting, logging — are free on any Cloudflare plan, with 10M persistent logs per gateway on Workers Paid, and no per-token markup verified. What it buys, in order of value here:

  • Per-workspace cost attribution. You cannot price a metered product you cannot measure per tenant. This is the reason to adopt it, and it is worth more than the caching.
  • Provider failover and one endpoint. Anthropic primary, Gemini and OpenRouter as fallbacks, without three SDKs and three retry policies in the worker.
  • Response caching for deterministic calls. Real hit rate on repeated prep over unchanged data; near zero on questions, which are all different. It complements provider-native prompt caching rather than replacing it — lever 2 above is Anthropic's, not the gateway's.
  • Rate limiting per workspace, which is how a fair-use cap gets enforced without writing a quota system.

Do not use its unified billing: that adds 5% on credit purchases, the same toll OpenRouter charges. Route through the gateway, pay the providers directly.

Adding Google as a model vendor is a governance decision, not just a cost one. The stack is deliberately Vercel, Cloudflare and Supabase, and every addition needs a written reason — cost is one, and it belongs in a design note, not in a commit message. Three checks come first: a data-processing agreement with zero retention on the paid tier; a subprocessor disclosure, because customer platform data crosses into a new vendor; and a read of the advertising platforms' own terms, several of which restrict transferring platform data to third parties outside a contracted service-provider relationship. The saving is worth having. It is not worth having quietly.

A Thai company buying American software

Taxed on both sides of the trade. Every vendor invoice arrives in dollars; every customer pays in baht. Handled properly the whole layer costs about 0.6% of foreign spend — handled carelessly, 9.5%, and the difference is administrative, not commercial.

What BOI promotion changes, and what it does not

A software company qualifies under BOI activity 8.1 — development of software, digital platforms or digital content, which carries an eight-year corporate income tax exemption, 100% foreign ownership, land rights and Smart Visa access, with no minimum registered capital and no four-Thai-per-foreigner ratio. The binding condition is that the development work is genuinely carried out in Thailand, and the minimum investment is around ฿1M excluding land and working capital. verified

Two things about it are widely misread, and both matter to this model.

  • The exemption is capped, and the cap is a payroll number. Under 8.1 the CIT exemption is limited to 100% of the salary of additional Thai IT personnel hired after applying, plus 200% of their training cost. This plan pays ฿11.4M of Thai engineering salary over 36 months against a maximum possible tax bill far below it, so the cap never binds — but it would bind on a company that outsourced its engineering, and it is a reason to apply before hiring rather than after.
  • BOI exempts corporate income tax and nothing else. The 7% import VAT of the table above, the withholding exposure, the 7% output VAT and every foreign-exchange cost are unaffected. §05 stands exactly as written under BOI.

In the 36-month window BOI is worth less than it sounds, because ordinary loss carry-forward already shelters most of it: Years 1 and 2 accumulate ฿10.8M of losses that offset Year 3’s profit, leaving a real tax bill of about ฿709,000 without promotion and zero with it. The value is in years four to eight, when the losses are used up and profit compounds — on this trajectory that is worth several times the whole peak funding requirement. Apply early, because the clock and the salary ceiling both start at approval, not at profitability.

Quoting VAT-inclusive at ฿1,490 rather than ฿1,590 would have cost 8% of contribution and moved break-even from 103 customers to 112; the extra hundred baht buys that back and still reads as a round number. Corporate income tax sits below all of this and outside a gross-margin model — Thailand taxes small companies on a graduated SME schedule rather than the headline 20%, and whether this company qualifies is a question for the accountant before the first profitable year, which on this plan is Year 3. assumed

The security hire, and a correction

An earlier version of this model argued there should be no IT function, on the grounds that a fully managed stack removes the servers, the load balancer, the queue and the backups. That part is still true — there is no infrastructure to administer and no ops hire. But it was the wrong conclusion, because the job that is needed here was never ops.

This product holds bring-your-own advertising and commerce credentials for thousands of businesses in one multi-tenant database, and the whole promise is per-workspace separation. The role that protects that is a security and data-protection engineer, and it is a different job from IT support: tenant isolation testing, secret and key rotation, access review, audit logging, incident response, subprocessor and vendor security assessments, and the PDPA record of processing that a customer’s own auditor will eventually ask for. It is modelled from month 10 — before the customer base makes a mistake expensive, and before the first enterprise security questionnaire arrives.

What PDPA actually requires

Thailand’s PDPA obliges a controller or processor to appoint a Data Protection Officer where core activities involve regular monitoring of personal data on a large scale — read in practice as 100,000 or more data subjects — or large-scale processing of sensitive data. Failing to appoint one when required carries an administrative fine of up to ฿1,000,000, and has been treated as an aggravating factor in calculating other fines. verified

On this trajectory the combined free and paying base, plus the end-customer records inside each workspace, crosses that threshold somewhere in year three. The DPO may be an employee or an external contractor; a contractor is both cheaper and cleaner, because a DPO must be able to act independently of the people whose processing they oversee. Modelled at ฿25,000 a month from month 24 — before the threshold rather than after it, because the fine is for not having appointed one, not for appointing one late.

Two design decisions already reduce this exposure and should be held to: contact data is hashed at the edge and never stored raw, and there is no cross-customer aggregation of platform data. Both narrow what counts as personal data on the platform, and both are worth more than any amount of policy documentation.

07 — Funnel and retention

Seventeen free users for every one who pays

At ฿1,590 a month, no human can profitably sell the entry tier. A single sales touch costs roughly ฿10,000 in loaded rep time against ฿1,211 of monthly contribution — eight months to repay the conversation alone. Starter and Growth have to convert without a human in the loop.

Human selling exists above them: Multi-unit through an account manager, and enterprise through the CEO. At ฿13,800 and ฿30,000+ a month those tickets carry a person easily, and the founder’s existing network is what makes the enterprise ramp credible at all. The ฿30,000 entry band matters here more than the headline numbers suggest — it is a contract a restaurant group can approve without a board paper, which is why the first one lands in month 14.

How many stay free

Almost all of them. Published freemium benchmarks put free-to-paid conversion at 2–5% as the median, with 3–5% good and 8–12% exceptional; across 200 B2B products the median is 4.5%, and a quarter convert below 2.5% verified. Freemium converts far worse than a trial — opt-in trials run 8–22%, card-required trials 35–55% — because a free plan has no deadline and no moment of decision.

This model assumes 4.3%, essentially the published median. About 96 in every 100 signups never pay. Because serving them costs ฿1.65 a head, that is a feature — though it is also what pushes the platform across the PDPA threshold in §06, which is the one cost the free tier genuinely carries.

The free tier is the acquisition channel

Thirty-five thousand free users cost ฿57,000 a month to serve. They are simultaneously the top of the funnel, the referral engine, the product-feedback surface, and the reason a Thai SME hears about this at all in a market with no category awareness. Every conventional SaaS instinct says to gate the data plane and force a trial clock; the cost structure in §01 says the opposite, and the cost structure is right.

Two costs it does carry, both named elsewhere: freemium converts worse than a trial, so layer a fourteen-day full-feature trial on top of the free plan rather than instead of it; and the free base is what pushes the platform past the PDPA large-scale threshold, which is a ฿25,000-a-month DPO retainer and a real compliance obligation, not a rounding error.

10 — Risk register

What this model does not know

Ranked by how much the answer moves the outcome. The first four are unvalidated commercial assumptions; nothing in the cost model can settle them and no amount of further modelling should be mistaken for trying.

  • Whether the CEO’s network converts into enterprise contracts. Twenty-five logos averaging ฿59,000 a month are 18% of month-36 revenue and pull break-even forward, and they rest entirely on one person’s relationships in Thai F&B. The ฿30,000 entry band makes the assumption more reachable than a ฿100,000 floor would — that is a contract a restaurant group approves without a board paper — but it does not make it evidence.
  • Whether a Thai SME pays ฿1,590 a month. The comparable anchors are accounting software at ฿199–999 and POS at ฿500–1,500. This price sits above both and is justified only if the after-fees number is one nobody else produces. The DEPA 200% deduction narrows the gap by 15–20% and expires at the end of 2027; five conversations settle the rest. assumed
  • Whether 58,000 Thai SMEs will open a free account in three years. The growth curve is a plan, not a forecast — it assumes a funnel that does not yet exist in a market with no category awareness, peaking at 3,700 signups a month.
  • Real churn, and real annual take-up. 4.5% monthly, 2.0% on annual and 1.0% on enterprise are the middle of published bands, not measurements, and 30% annual take-up is a convention. Between the pessimistic and optimistic outlooks the terminal ARR differs threefold with no change to anything else. Both are visible by month 9. benchmark verified
  • Free-to-paid conversion. 4.3% is the published freemium median, and a quarter of freemium products convert below 2.5%. At 2.5% the CAC rises to ฿14,300 — through the ceiling — and the paid channel has to be switched off entirely, which is the pessimistic outlook. benchmark verified
  • Whether SOC 2 is actually required, and when. ฿400,000 of readiness plus ฿27,400 a month of tooling is budgeted from year two on the assumption that E2 and E3 buyers demand it. If the first ten enterprise buyers accept a security questionnaire and a penetration-test report instead, that money is saved for a year. If a single large buyer demands ISO 27001 as well, it doubles. This is the widest-variance line in §04.
  • Support load per paying customer. Modelled at one hire per 400. For owner-operated businesses with no in-house analyst that ratio may be optimistic, and support is the line most likely to turn a 76% gross margin into a 45% operating one. Free users generate support load too, and this model charges them none.
  • Whether BOI promotion is granted, and when. Activity 8.1 requires that development genuinely happens in Thailand and carries an application process measured in months. The exemption ceiling is cumulative Thai IT salary paid after approval, so applying late permanently shrinks it. In this 36-month window BOI is worth nothing at all — carried-forward losses shelter year three completely — and in years four to eight it is worth many times the entire raise. verified
  • Enterprise scope creep, and the floor holding. The band margins in §02 assume a fixed scope per band; a customer who negotiates E1 pricing with E3 requirements is a different product sold at a loss. The ฿25,000 break-even is the number to hold, and the temptation to sign below it will be strongest exactly when the pipeline is thin.
  • Marketplace and partner terms. No published API fee has been found for Shopee, Lazada or LINE MAN Wongnai, and partner programmes may carry commercial terms. If distribution ever runs through an app store, a 15% revenue share is larger than the entire infrastructure bill. assumed

And what is still not in the model at all

None of these change the shape of the answer, but each is a real number somebody will eventually have to write down. They are listed so that the omission is a decision rather than an oversight.

  • Thai severance accrual. Labour law requires severance on termination, rising with length of service. A prudent provision is 2–4% of payroll — roughly ฿400,000–800,000 a year by year three. It is a liability rather than a cash cost until somebody leaves, which is exactly why companies forget it.
  • Equipment refresh. Laptops are expensed at hire here; a three-year cycle means the first refresh lands in year four, around ฿700,000.
  • A second office. The 200 sqm lease is sized for nineteen people at 10.5 sqm each. The optimistic outlook does not need more space, but a fourth year at that growth rate does — and a mid-lease move means a second fit-out and an overlap period.
  • Work permits and visas for any non-Thai founder or hire. BOI promotion makes this materially easier and cheaper, which is a second reason to apply early.
  • ESOP. Setting up an option pool has legal cost and, more importantly, dilution that no operating model shows. Decide the pool size before the raise, not after.
  • Directors’ and officers’ liability insurance, usually requested by an institutional investor at the point of the round rather than before it.
  • Marketing production — video, photography, Thai-language content at volume. Budgeted only inside the blended CAC, which understates it if the content channel becomes the primary one.
  • Foreign-exchange hedging. Revenue is entirely in baht and roughly a fifth of cost is in dollars. A 10% baht depreciation adds about ฿1.2M a year to the year-three cost base. The ×1.006 uplift in §05 covers conversion spread, not currency risk.
  • Deferred revenue. Annual and enterprise contracts create a balance-sheet liability that grows to roughly ฿9M by month 36. It costs nothing, but it is the difference between the cash line in §08 and what an auditor will call distributable.
11 — Position and verdict

Everything above this section is arithmetic. This one is judgement: where a product like this sits in the Thai market in September 2026, what the market is currently handing it for free, and what it could take back.

What could take it away

Business risks distinct from the modelling uncertainties in §10 judgement

The aggregator localisesHighest likelihood, highest impact Supermetrics or Windsor already carry Shopee and Lazada connectors. If one prices for Southeast Asia and ships Thai-language onboarding, the connector advantage disappears in a quarter. Do not compete on connector count. The defensible product is the after-fees number, the restatement correctness and the advice layer — none of which an aggregator sells, because their customer is an analyst, not an owner.
Platform permission risk Every connector is a permission granted by Meta, Google, Shopee or Lazada and revocable by them. App Review, business verification and sensitive-scope review are gates you do not control, and a single policy change can remove a source overnight. Bring-your-own-credential everywhere the terms require it, no shared platform tokens, per-workspace separation, and the Meta client-list obligation designed in from the start. It is why those constraints are treated as design inputs rather than paperwork.
Nobody is searching for this The plan needs 58,000 free signups in a market with no category awareness, peaking at 3,700 a month. This is not a product problem and no amount of engineering fixes it. Trade shows, the free tier as the entire top of funnel, accountant and agency referral, and a CEO whose network substitutes for the awareness that does not exist yet.
Thai SME mortality Churn benchmarks from Western SaaS do not capture the base rate at which small Thai restaurants and retailers simply close. Some of the 4.5% is not a product failure and cannot be fixed by a better product. Annual billing removes eleven cancellation decisions a year and is the only lever that works on churn you did not cause. It is also why the Multi-unit and enterprise mix matters — larger customers close less often.
The ฿1,590 ceiling Accounting software anchors at ฿199–999 and POS at ฿500–1,500. If the after-fees number is not visibly worth more than both combined, the whole ARPU stack, and therefore the whole plan, resets downward. The DEPA deduction closes 15–20% of the gap through 2027. Beyond that, only the product does — which is the real reason the routing engine and the action sheet matter more than any cost line.
Model pricing and capability drift Every margin here assumes September 2026 token prices. Prices have fallen historically, which helps — but the advice layer is a single vendor’s frontier model, and a price change or capability regression moves COGS directly. Cloudflare AI Gateway with Anthropic primary and Gemini plus OpenRouter as failover; the routing engine, which cuts exposure by two thirds; and batching, which is vendor-agnostic.
Talent competition Bangkok senior engineers are contested by multinationals paying up to ฿234,000. This plan pays ฿120,000 plus a fourteen-month package. Equity, a real office, genuine ownership of a correctness problem, and ฿200-a-month tooling. It is a real constraint on hiring speed, not on whether hiring is possible.
Founder concentration 18% of month-36 revenue depends on one person’s relationships; the routing engine depends on one person’s execution. Neither is diversified in a three-person founding team. Nothing, honestly, inside three years. It is a fact of the shape rather than a problem with a mitigation, and it belongs in the conversation with an investor rather than buried.
Data-residency tightening If PDPA enforcement or an enterprise buyer forces Thai-hosted data, the Cloudflare and Supabase cost structure — and the zero-egress economics behind the free tier — no longer applies. Watch it rather than pre-build for it. A pinned data region is already an E3 line item, so the first customer who needs it pays for finding out.
The twenty-four-month plateau The company needs ฿19.3M, does not turn cash-positive inside the window, and every month of delay compounds against a fixed lease and a fixed team. Annual billing, the enterprise entry band collecting up front, and raising ฿24–26M rather than the minimum. Under-raising is the most common way a plan like this fails, and it fails at month 22, not month 6.

The verdict

This is a real business and a slow one. The unit economics are not in question: 76% gross margin, flat across scale, on a cost base that has now been loaded with a lease, a fit-out, an auditor and a penetration tester and still holds. What is in question is entirely commercial — whether Thai SMEs will pay ฿1,590 for a number nobody currently produces for them, and whether 58,000 of them can be reached in a market with no category.

The shape is unusual and worth naming plainly. It has the margin profile of software and the capital profile of a small services company: ฿19.3M in, no cash-positive month inside three years, and ฿92M of ARR at the end of it. It is not a venture-scale outcome on this trajectory and it is not trying to be — but the optimistic case reaches ฿132.6M of ARR on ฿14.5M of capital, and the downside is bounded by a cheque rather than by a structural flaw.

Three things decide which outlook you land in, and none of them is in this document. Retention, which is visible by month nine and which annual billing is the only real lever on. Category creation, which the free tier and the trade-show floor have to do together because paid search cannot create demand that does not exist. And the CEO’s network, which converts into an enterprise line worth 18% of revenue or it does not. The cost model is settled. The commercial assumptions are not, and five customer conversations are worth more than another revision of this page.

One internal decision is large enough to change the answer inside any outlook: ship the routing engine in §03 before scaling acquisition. It more than doubles year-three EBITDA in the normal case, turns the pessimistic case profitable, is worth ฿1.8M of the raise, and is work the product requires anyway. Everything else in this document is a number to be measured rather than a choice to be made.
